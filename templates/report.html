METHODOLOGY

Qualification Criteria

Batting Qualification

A player must meet the minimum batting participation requirements for a season to be included in batting analysis.

Minimum Innings 7 innings
Minimum Balls Faced 120 balls

Bowling Qualification

A player must meet the minimum bowling participation requirements for a season to be included in bowling analysis.

Minimum Innings 7 innings
Minimum Balls Bowled 120 balls

Why are qualification criteria used?

Minimum participation requirements prevent players with very small sample sizes from receiving misleading performance scores. Only qualifying seasons are included in performance indices, charts, profiles, and detailed statistical analysis.
